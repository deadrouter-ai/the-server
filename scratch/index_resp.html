Frequently Conjured Queries

Quid separant hoc ritum ab OpenRouter? (DeadRouter vs. OpenRouter)

OpenRouter operates with a corporate agenda: they offer a vast catalog of 900+ models, corporate limits, and a highly polished corporate UI to satisfy enterprise boards. However, their operations remain anchored to terrestrial logging and surveillance.

DeadRouter has a different priority: we curate the best selection of the most private and secure provider sanctuaries available. Some of our selected providers might exist on corporate routers; others are custom, hidden nodes you will not find anywhere else. OpenRouter caters to corporate limits; we offer unbeatable, absolute privacy.

Why is your list of AI spirits (models) so small compared to others?

We do not believe in quantity over quality. While other gateways boast hundreds of copycat models and fine-tunes, most are corporate junk designed to harvest user prompts for future training.

We curate our list of AI spirits with extreme care, only offering models that are either open-weights and run on verified confidential infrastructure, or support Zero Data Retention (ZDR) and Zero Data Scanning (ZDS) natively. We supply only the strongest, most secure spirits.

Oblivion (ZDR) vs. Blindness (ZDS): A pinky promise or a locked gate?

We pioneered the concept of Zero Data Scanning (ZDS) because Zero Data Retention (ZDR) has become a corporate marketing loophole. Under standard ZDR, a provider promises to delete your prompt *after* generation, but they still scan, classify, and inspect your messages in transit.

For instance, platforms like OpenRouter explicitly reserve the right to scan and categorize incoming queries. Even if they promise not to link these classification logs to your account, the scan still occurs—and their "unlinkability" claims can instantly collapse under judicial pressure. Furthermore, downstream leviathans like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google actively deploy automated "trust and safety" classifiers. If your queries trigger these invisible wards, their policies grant them the power to proactively forward your IP address and prompt data to law enforcement—often based merely on their own internal "good faith" assessment, without requiring a court order. ZDS removes this vulnerability by ensuring the downstream provider is completely blinded in transit or runs within verifiable hardware secure enclaves, rendering scanning mathematically impossible.

What are these AMD SEV-SNP "Confidential Enclaves" you speak of?

Our gateway runs inside AMD SEV-SNP (Secure Encrypted Virtualization-Secure Nested Paging) enclaves. This means your requests are processed in cryptographically isolated memory regions.

Even if the physical server hosting our gateway is compromised, or the hosting provider attempts to dump the RAM, the CPU hardware itself encrypts the memory with a key generated on-chip. No hypervisor, root user, or terrestrial magistrate can view the plain-text data flowing through our enclaves.

How much privacy can I obtain through this gateway?

We establish a clear, uncompromised line between convenience and absolute privacy, offering two distinct account structures:

  • Zero Logs Accounts (Maximum Sanctity): Created with an opaque, random identifier. All account state (except for the unlinked balance token) is client-side or securely encrypted. In this mode, you must tithe exclusively with cryptocurrencies; fiat payments are strictly barred. We never log transactions or query history. Once a response is returned to you, our database retains no record of the exchange. We cannot mathematically prove a specific request ever took place. A one-click deletion instantly purges your API keys and login tokens from the server (with a 7-day automated backup recovery window).
  • Normal Accounts (Convenience-Oriented): Supports both cryptocurrency and fiat card payments. Due to strict anti-abuse rules imposed by fiat card networks, we must retain transaction metadata. If you top up via credit/debit card, we store the timestamp, transaction ID, payment value, and account association. When you perform queries, we do not save the prompt text, but we do log the metadata: timestamp, model, tokens consumed, balance changes, and account links. Consequently, if law enforcement requests information regarding a specific downstream transaction or account, we can and will release this metadata. However, we never collect IP addresses, device fingerprints, or browser parameters under any circumstances. Upon deletion, normal accounts are fully purged after 90 days to prevent dispute fraud and payment abuse.

If your work demands ultimate secrecy, we highly encourage using a Zero Logs account capped with cryptographic payments.

What happens when I click the "Purge Account" button?

If you are on a Zero Logs Account, clicking "Purge" instantly dissolves all records: your API keys and opaque login tokens are wiped immediately (with a 7-day automated backup recovery window).

For Normal Accounts, the metadata of your payment transactions must remain in quarantine for 90 days after deletion to comply with payment processor anti-abuse policies. If you value complete and instant dissolution, stick to Zero Logs and crypto tithes.

Do you support No-JS, Tor, and privacy browsers?

Yes. DeadRouter is one of the only AI API gateways built from the ground up to support a pure No-JS experience. We operate a dedicated Tor Onion Service (.onion) that permits you to register, top up, and perform API queries from the shadows.

The entire interface is optimized and thoroughly tested on the Tor Browser under its "Safest" security preset (which disables all JavaScript execution), ensuring no tracking scripts can ever run on your machine.
