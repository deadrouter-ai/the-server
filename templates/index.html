Frequently Asked Questions

OpenRouter vs DeadRouter?

DeadRouter is a "Router for Routers." We actually include access to OpenRouter, Nano-GPT, and others. The core difference is anonymization. We mask your identity, offer .onion access, enforce strict ZDS (Zero Data Scanning), allow token-based anonymous accounts, and accept cryptocurrency with zero logs. You use their models, but with our extreme privacy shield.

What is the difference between ZDR and ZDS?

ZDR (Zero Data Retention) simply means a provider drops your prompt after generation. However, many ZDR providers still scan your prompts in transit and will override their own ZDR policies to log your data if they flag you. We introduced ZDS (Zero Data Scanning) to guarantee that your data is not just deleted, but actively blocked from being scanned or flagged in the first place.

What information is required to create an account?

Literally none. We do not ask for an email, a name, or a phone number. Upon clicking "Generate Account", you are given a mathematically secure login token. Keep that token safe; it is your only way to access your account. We process traditional payments via Stripe, but heavily encourage our in-house, zero-log cryptocurrency payment gateway.

Is the site fully functional without JavaScript?

Yes. DeadRouter is 100% No-JS friendly. We believe true privacy advocates should be able to browse, create accounts, read documentation, and utilize our web interfaces without needing to execute a single line of client-side JavaScript.

How does End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) work here?

For compatible downstream providers, we allow you to encrypt your payloads client-side using their public keys. The prompt routes through our servers (masking your IP and account data from them), but remains entirely unreadable to DeadRouter. We just route the encrypted blob, effectively terminating your trust in us.
